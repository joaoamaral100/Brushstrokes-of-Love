You can begin where you are

A little curiosity is
a good place to start.

  • You love your dog and would like to make something personal.
  • You are new to watercolour and want a clear visual path.
  • You like the idea of a creative hobby you can return to.
  • You want a handmade keepsake, a picture for your home or a thoughtful gift.

You don’t need art-school experience or a large paint collection. You will need suitable watercolour paper, paints, brushes, a few basic tools and a clear printed photograph.

Practise on spare paper, take breaks and let the layers dry. The guide is there to help you continue, one manageable decision at a time.

Before you choose your photo

A few questions,
answered clearly.

Which version should I choose?+

Choose Essential (£14.90) if you want the complete core path from dog photograph to watercolour portrait. Choose Complete (£24.90) for that same guide plus the joint portrait, wall-art and child activity guides, and the three printable companion packs.

Are the gallery pictures student results?+

No. The gallery transformations and lifestyle scenes are original illustrative examples. They show possible directions, not verified student results or a promise of an identical painting. Product previews show the real digital guides.

Do I need to know how to draw?+

No. The guide shows you how to transfer the important outlines from a printed photograph onto watercolour paper. You still learn to observe the shapes and markings in your own photograph, but you do not have to draw the whole portrait freehand.

Is this suitable for a complete beginner?+

Yes. Start with the materials and brush-loading pages, practise a few washes, then work through the portrait. The practical pages explain the action, show the intended result, cover a common mistake and give a signal for when to continue. You can repeat a practice before moving on.

Do I use my own dog photo?+

Yes. The guide helps you choose a clear photograph with visible eyes, useful light and a manageable angle. A consistent worked example shows the process; you compare each decision with your own dog’s proportions, colours and markings.

What art materials will I need?+

Watercolour paper, watercolour paints, round brushes, a palette, clean water, a cloth or kitchen roll, a pencil, low-tack tape and a printed photograph. For the transfer method, you also need suitable transfer paper. Materials, printing and framing are not included.

Do I need expensive paints?+

You do not need a large collection or a premium brand to begin. The guide works with a small palette and explains mixing and water balance. Suitable watercolour paper and a brush that holds a point will help you practise the techniques.

Is this a physical book?+

No. Both options are digital. Essential includes the 50-page main PDF; Complete adds the three bonus guides and three printable extra packs. The books, printed sheets, tablets, frames and art materials in the illustrations show ways to use the guides; they are not shipped with your purchase.

Can I print the guide?+

Yes, you can print the pages you want to follow or practise from for your own use. You can also keep the PDF on screen and print only your photograph and any reference sheets you need. Printing costs and supplies are separate.

What if my dog has black fur?+

The main guide includes a page on black fur: look for lighter reflections and preserve differences between the dark shapes. Use it alongside the value and layering lessons rather than filling the whole coat with one flat dark wash.

What if my dog has white fur?+

There is a dedicated white-fur page. It shows the role of untouched paper and pale shadow shapes, so you can suggest form while keeping the coat light.

What if my dog has long or curly fur?+

The guide covers both. Long fur is approached as groups moving in a direction; curly fur is approached through grouped shapes and spaces of light. You apply those ideas to the fur in your own photograph.

Which option includes the bonuses and extras?+

Essential (£14.90) includes only the 50-page main guide. Complete (£24.90) includes that same main guide, Paint a Portrait of You & Your Dog, Turn Your Dog Portrait Into Wall Art, Paint Your Dog With Your Child, plus Quick Start Companion, Portrait Planning Pack and Share & Gift Pack. Optional paid practice add-ons at checkout are separate from both options.

Can I use the method for another dog later?+

Yes. The photo-selection, transfer, observation and painting process can be used again with another dog photograph. Adjust the shapes, markings and coat treatment to suit that dog.

Can I follow it on a tablet?+

Yes. Use a PDF reader that lets you zoom into the visual steps. A tablet, laptop or desktop can sit beside your painting space; keep devices safely away from water. No device is included.

How do I receive access?+

The checkout platform provides the delivery and access instructions for the digital files. Check the information shown there when ordering and follow the instructions provided after purchase. No physical delivery is involved.

Is there a video course or live teaching?+

Both options use illustrated PDF guides. It is designed to be followed at your own pace, with written actions, visual examples and checks. Video lessons and live tuition are not part of this bundle.
